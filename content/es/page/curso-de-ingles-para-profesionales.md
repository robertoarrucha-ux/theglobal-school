# Inglés para Profesionales

**NIVEL INTERMEDIO Y AVANZADO**

**Unit 1: Business Language Basics**

- Introduction to Business English vocabulary
- Common phrases and idioms in a professional setting
- Basic grammar review

**Unit 2: Professional Writing**

- Best practices for writing professional emails and memos
- Crafting compelling presentations and reports in English
- Identifying and rectifying common errors

**Unit 3: Business Communication Skills**

- Techniques for clear and effective speech in professional conversations and meetings
- Listening skills for understanding and responding appropriately

**Unit 4: Project Management Vocabulary**

- Terms and phrases commonly used in project planning and execution
- Understanding requests, giving instructions and providing updates.

**Unit 5: Language for Negotiation**

- Vocabulary and phrases for negotiating contract terms, prices, and more
- Simulated negotiations for practice

**Unit 6: Customer Service Language**

- Phrases and techniques for achieving customer satisfaction
- Managing complaints and providing assistance in English

**Unit 7: Vocabulary for Specific Sectors**

- Terminology used in major industries such as Finance, Marketing, Tech, Engineering, etc.

**Unit 8: Networking and Socializing**

- Conversation skills for professional networking events
- Culturally appropriate small talk

Each unit will contain a mix of individual study exercises, group activities, role-plays, and quizzes to ensure a comprehensive understanding and application of the learned material.

The course will end with a final exam to gauge progress and areas of improvement.
