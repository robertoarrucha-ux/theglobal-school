# International Events & Educational Programmes

**Since 2010, Vienna, Berlin & Geneva.**

### **Paid Internships**

### **Youtuber**

At The New Global School, you will have the opportunity to work in a vibrant and award-winning international organisation.

**We design, promote and organize international events and educational programmes for top organizations and companies, mainly in Europe, but also across some capitals of the world.**

This internship will allow you to join efforts in creating educational video content, analysing topics of leadership, geopolitics, sustainable development, business, and innovation.

If you are a creative and motivated individual looking to advance your career in editorial analysis, communication, social media, marketing, and community building, we have a place for you!

**Responsibilities**

- Assist in the creation and execution of educational and analytical content on YouTube.
- Help us build a strong and informed community around our brand.
- Assist in managing and growing the YouTube presence.
- Collaborate with other departments to ensure brand consistency and create special content.
- Stay updated with the latest trends and best practices on YouTube.

**Requirements**

- Passion and curiosity for analysis, critical thinking, and communication.
- Ability to process and filter information.
- Mastery of simple, engaging, and understandable writing.
- Experience with YouTube Creators and YouTube Analytics.
- Desired but not mandatory experience in digital graphic design.
- Strong organisational and communication skills.
- Attention to detail, quality, and rigor of content.
- Proficiency in English at an upper-intermediate level, capable of conducting research and translations.
- Knowledge of digital marketing tools and SEO is an advantage.

**Benefits**

At The New Global School, we believe in fostering a culture of growth and development.  
As an analysis and content creation intern, you will have the unique opportunity to learn and grow in a professional, fun, and fast-paced environment. In addition to that, we offer:

- A dynamic and diverse international team from more than 11 different countries.  
  Access to permanent job opportunities with The New Global School or with our international partners.
- You will learn skills in digital productivity tools, project management, and teamwork.
- **A flexible remote work schedule of 15 hours per week.**
- The New Global School membership to enjoy all international programmes and events for FREE.
- Remuneration of: €300 to €500 monthly depending on skills and results.

**Selection Process:**

- Send your CV and Portafolio to: [***hello@theglobal.school,*** specify for the position you are applying for.](mailto:hello@theglobal.school)
- Selected candidates will undergo a creation test where they must develop 2 videos on pre-selected topics.
- Each candidate will have access to digital tools for creating their videos, as well as some tips and guidelines.
- After creating and submitting their video, our team will publish it on YouTube to measure results.
- The candidate with the best video results will be selected.  
  Results are based on the following criteria:  
  -> Content quality  
  -> Topic relevance  
  -> Duration  
  -> Views, retention, clicks, and desired audience.

Application Deadline: Open all the year.
