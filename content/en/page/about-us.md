# International Events & Education

---

We design, promote and organize international events & educational programs for companies and organizations.

**Since 2010, Vienna, Berlin & Geneva**

### **Who are we?**

---

We are a multi-awarded NGO dedicated to the design, promotion and organization of international events and educational programs such as **Seminars, congresses, field studies, diplomatic & trade missions, awards & recognitions, training, certifications, and educational trips, as well as online communities and digital solutions to event organizers and educational providers.**

Established in 2010, our operations extend across prominent locations, including Vienna, Berlin, Geneva and Mexico City.

Our work has been recognized by the United Nations Regional Academy and awarded by the Government of Austria and the Chamber of Commerce. We have also received hundreds of positive ratings.

**The New Global School is an independent, self-funded, non-governmental organization not aligned with any political agenda or ideology.**

### **How do we do it?**

![Social Innovation Box](/media/2022/04/Latam-Summit-OFID-WEB-2.webp "Latam-Summit-OFID-WEB-2")

The New Global School [**partners with organisations, companies, & academies**](https://theglobal.school/global-consultant/) to design, organize and run international events and educational programs.

**Our Events and Programs offer a unique life experience that allows you to access top knowledge, tools, network, technology, and alliances worldwide.**

Our methodology is built upon 10 years of work experience**around the world.**
