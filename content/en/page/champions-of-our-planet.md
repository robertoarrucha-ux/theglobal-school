## What?

Champions of Our Planet is the “**TEDx for Climate Business”**, where you will get inspired by the amazing stories of success. 

Champions of Our Planet is part of the **Discover Vienna Week: Climate Tech**, where you will have the chance to meet climate tech and businesses from all over Europe who gather in Vienna.

We connect and recognise the most innovative and boldest businesses working towards sustainable development and a liveable future. You can still apply, [click here.](https://gozenforms.com/6fPn6bINJBSFmA8fS63A)

## Who?

##### What is the Discovery Vienna Climate Tech?

To facilitate exchange and knowledge transfer between the stakeholders of the vibrant impact scene in Vienna and international startups, Vienna Business Agency host a five-day program from November 5 to November 10, 2023.

**Discover Vienna: Climate Tech** will provide insights into the Viennese Climate Tech ecosystem and can help you to explore business opportunities in Vienna. The programme focuses primarily on the fields of Renewable Energy, Hydrogen, Climate Change Adaption, Circular Economy, Sustainable Mobility, and Urban Logistics.

Previous to the program, there will be the International Vienna Energy and Climate Forum ([IVECF](https://www.ivecf.org/)) organized during 2-3 November in the Hofburg Imperial Palace by our partner [UNIDO](https://www.unido.org/).
