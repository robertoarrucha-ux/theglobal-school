## **Educational Programs for Leaders**

We design and organise executive programs for public & private sector leaders: **Trade Missions, Recognitions, Training, Workshops, Consulting, Educational & Business Tours, Business and International Alliances**.

We specialise in 3 themes: **Sustainable Development, Innovation and Leadership.**

**We are a non-profit educational organisation.** Our programs have been recognised by the United Nations Organization, and the University of Oxford, and awarded by 1MillionStartups in partnership with the Government of Austria.
