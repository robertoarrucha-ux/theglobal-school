# **Who are we?**

Since 2010, we’ve helped companies, governments and organizations **increase their market share, attract investment** and **solidify their brand,** through missions, events, and education:

**Summits, Congresses, Fairs, Field Studies, Seminars, Trade Missions, Awards, Workshops, Certifications, etc.**

Our programs have been **recognised** by dozens of organisations worldwide such as the United Nations, the Government of Austria and Mexico, and the University of Oxford.
