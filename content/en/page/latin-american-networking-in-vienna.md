# Latin American Networking Vienna 2025

**December 5th, 2025**

Join us in the 6th edition of the Latin American Networking Night in Vienna.

**Latin American Startups, Businesses, Investors and Governments are visiting Vienna to explore partnerships and alliances.**

**Who should attend?**  
Anyone exploring business opportunities in new markets.

**What can you expect?**

- Meet professionals and entrepreneurs from both sides of the Atlantic.
- Expand your network and open doors to new opportunities.
- A warm, vibrant environment where connections and business flow naturally.

### **Agenda**

**Casual/Formal are both acceptable.** COMPULSORY: do not dress informal.

**06:00 pm.** We start with a toast and ice-breaker at [*District Living. Donau-City-Straße 3, 1220 Wien, Austria.*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/D8UgY8Hty3UpSQoB7)

**08:00 pm.** Party time. Enjoy a wonderful evening/night with dances, games and fun networking activities. [*District Living. Donau-City-Straße 3, 1220 Wien, Austria.*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/D8UgY8Hty3UpSQoB7)

### **Entry Options**

**1. FREE:** Enjoy a wonderful evening/night with amazing networking activities.

**2. FOOD & DRINKS.** Enjoy traditional empanadas and tacos from our amazing vendors. **You can pay on the spot.**

You are having an amazing night filled with activities, games, and entertainment.

**Do you need to pay to attend the event?**

**FREE of charge. You pay for your drinks and food.**

### **Why should you attend?**

- 600 million market in one single language.
- Latin America is considered a critical piece on the new geo-economic chessboard
- Vast and full of vital resources for addressing food security & climate change.
- Dominant producer of one of the world´s most important minerals: Lithium.
- Early and eager adopters of new digital products and services.
- Global leaders in building an innovative, inclusive financial system.
- 2 of the worlds top 6 economies by 2050.
- A lot of experience dealing with economic challenges.
- Top 5 carbon sink; Top 3 food producer; Top Lithium producer.
- Young & digitally native.
- New manufacturing global hub
- Reliable, purpose-driven and professional partners.
- Beautiful beaches, welcoming culture, delicious food, stunning landscapes…. and we know how to have fun!
