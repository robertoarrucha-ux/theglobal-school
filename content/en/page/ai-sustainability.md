## What?

The AI European Forum is a space to connect, meet and match with AI startups, companies, organizations, researchers and enthusiasts, in an effort to boost a vibrant ecosystem of collaboration and opportunities.

**Promising** startups and companies will present their solutions through out our Kick Off event on the 10th and 11th of October.

##### MORE DETAILS

The AI European Forum serves as a dynamic hub designed to foster connections among a diverse array of stakeholders, including AI startups, established companies, research organisations, and enthusiasts. The primary goal of the forum is to catalyse a vibrant ecosystem that encourages collaboration, innovation, and the exploration of new opportunities within the field of artificial intelligence.

### Why should you attend the AI European Forum:

1. **Networking Opportunities**:

   - The forum provides a platform for participants to connect with like-minded individuals and organisations.
   - Events such as workshops, panel discussions, and informal meet-ups facilitate relationship building and knowledge sharing.
2. **Showcasing Innovations**:

   - Startups and established companies have the opportunity to showcase their latest AI technologies and solutions.
   - Demonstrations and presentations allow participants to gain insights into cutting-edge developments in the field.
3. **Collaborative Projects**:

   - The forum encourages partnerships between startups and larger corporations, as well as collaborations between academia and industry.
   - Initiatives can arise from these partnerships, leading to innovative projects that address real-world challenges.
4. **Research and Development**:

   - Researchers can present their findings and engage with industry leaders to translate theoretical work into practical applications.
   - Discussions on ethical AI, data governance, and best practices contribute to responsible innovation.
5. **Sustainability Focus**:

   - The forum places a strong emphasis on sustainability in AI development. This includes discussions on how AI can contribute to sustainable practices across various sectors.
   - Topics such as energy-efficient algorithms, responsible data usage, and the environmental impact of AI systems are central to the dialogue.
   - Participants are encouraged to explore how AI can help tackle global challenges like climate change, resource management, and social equity.
6. **Educational Workshops**:

   - The forum also offers workshops aimed at increasing knowledge about AI technologies and their applications.
   - These sessions cover topics ranging from machine learning basics to advanced applications of AI in sustainable development.
7. **Future Trends**:

   - Experts share insights into future trends in AI, including the integration of sustainability into technological advancement.
   - Discussions on regulatory frameworks and policies help shape a responsible approach to AI innovation.

### 

[The AI European Forum](https://theglobal.school/about-us/) is not just a meeting point; it is a catalyst for growth and collaboration. By connecting diverse stakeholders and focusing on sustainable practices, the forum aims to contribute significantly to the development of an ethical and innovative AI landscape in Europe. Through shared knowledge and resources, participants can work together towards a future where AI technology aligns with global sustainability goals.
